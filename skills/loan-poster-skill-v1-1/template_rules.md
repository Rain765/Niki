# Template Rules

## Composition

- Respect the requested `1:1` square, `16:9` landscape, or `3:4` portrait canvas.
- Keep the headline and subline on the left and the main model on the right.
- Render one centered, large, low-opacity Z inside the unified poster image.
- The Z must read as part of the background and remain behind every model,
  phone, headline, subtitle, and logo pixel.
- Never add the Z as a post-generation overlay to a flattened full poster.
- Use a red-based gradient as the dominant field.
- Add one glossy curved ribbon along the bottom.

## People and scene

- Use realistic African / Black models in authentic Zambian lifestyle scenes.
- Keep the main model's phone clearly visible and anatomically credible.
- Vary age, relationship, occupation, setting, and model grouping across a batch.
- Use premium commercial lighting and natural skin retouching.

## Phone interface

- Use a white screen, one solid green checkmark, and short black status text.
- Keep the interface simple enough to remain legible at poster scale.

## Brand marks

- The selected brand controls art direction and output naming.
- Do not place `Zamloan`, `Biu Money`, `BiuMoney`, or another standalone
  brand-name wordmark in the top-left corner.
- Do not add a replacement icon logo, small corner badge, or duplicate Z.
- The single semi-transparent background Z is the only permitted brand mark.
