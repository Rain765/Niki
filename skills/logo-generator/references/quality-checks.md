# Logo quality checks

Use this checklist when comparing concepts and before exporting a selected direction.

## Recognition

- The silhouette remains recognizable at 16 px and when blurred slightly.
- The mark has one dominant idea that can be described in a sentence.
- Alternatives differ in concept, not merely styling.

## Production

- Verify one-color black, one-color white, and transparent-background variants.
- Check light and dark backgrounds and avoid hairline details that disappear in print.
- Build horizontal, stacked, symbol-only, and favicon lockups when the selected architecture supports them.
- Convert the selected simple geometry to SVG with clean paths, a sensible viewBox, and no embedded raster image.
- Record colors as HEX and typography with licensing notes.

## Risk review

- Search for close silhouettes in the same market before presenting a final recommendation.
- Check initials, negative space, and rotated forms for unintended symbols or offensive readings.
- Avoid flags, sacred symbols, government seals, and culturally specific motifs unless the brief explicitly calls for them and their use is appropriate.
- Describe the review as a preliminary similarity check, not legal or trademark clearance.
